About Me

I'm Tanveer, a Full Stack Developer who enjoys the whole journey of a product — from the first sketch to the final pixel.

Process

My Design & Development Process

A clear, proven path from the first conversation to a finished product.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand goals, users, requirements, and the real problems worth solving.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Research, define scope, structure the content, and build clear wireframes.

  3. 03

    Design

    Create a clean, consistent, user-focused interface that builds trust at a glance.

  4. 04

    Develop

    Build responsive, accessible, and performant interfaces with clean code.

  5. 05

    Test

    Test usability, responsiveness, accessibility, and cross-browser behavior.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Deploy the final product, document what matters, and provide ongoing support.
